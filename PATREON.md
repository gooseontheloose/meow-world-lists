# MEOW - Kitty Lounge: Patreon / VIP plan

All prices are USD per month. Perks stack: every tier includes everything below it.

| Tier | Price | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| **Kitten** | $5 | Supporter role in Discord, name on the in-world Supporters board (coming), our love |
| **Cozy Cat VIP** | $12 | VIP role on join + the **overhead VIP paw flair** (they can hide it any time in Settings), VIP area (when it opens), may give plain VIP to friends (friends get no flair / music) |
| **DJ VIP** | $25 | Everything above + **music control**: the DJ tab (house music, play now / queue, Twitch / YouTube / SoundCloud links, level for everyone), priority booking for DJ sets |
| **Poster Patron** | $50 | Everything above + **one big VIP poster** in the courtyard for the month (lit, framed), 1 free design swap per month |

Add-ons (any tier): extra poster slot +$35/mo - mid-month poster swap $10 - **Event Sponsor $100/mo**: two front-wall posters + a shout-out at every event.
Yearly: pay 10 months, get 12.

## How perks are given (manual, ~1 day)
1. Patron joins on Patreon (link Discord so the bot gives the Discord role).
2. Add their **exact VRChat display name** to `roster.json` -> `"vips"` (DJ VIP and up). Cozy Cat VIP uses the same list for now.
3. Poster Patrons: send the poster -> save it as `posters/poster_NN.png` (see posters/README.md). It shows on everyone's next join - no world upload.
4. Cancelled? Remove them at the end of the paid month.

## Rules for VIPs
- Perks follow the world rules; staff can remove any perk for rule breaks (no refund for the current month).
- Posters must follow the VRChat Terms of Service and Community Guidelines: no nudity, gore, hate or illegal content, no scams or links to them.
- Check VRChat's current Terms and creator guidelines on paid perks before launch; keep perks cosmetic / convenience (no pay-to-moderate).

## What the world owner has
Everything: Owner rank (all moderation), Host, Security, DJ, Video, gate Access and the VIP flair (hideable in Settings).
